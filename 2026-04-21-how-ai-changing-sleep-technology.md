# How AI Is Changing Sleep Technology: From Tracking to Intervention

---

For decades, sleep tracking has been the norm in consumer sleep tech. But AI is changing the game — we're moving from just *knowing* about your sleep to actually *improving* it.

As founders building an AI-powered sleep device, we've had a front-row seat to this shift. Let's break down what's changing, and why it matters for everyone who cares about better sleep.

---

## The Old Model: Tracking Only Tells You You Have a Problem

If you've used a popular fitness tracker, you know the routine:
- You wake up
- It gives you a "sleep score"
- It tells you you slept poorly
- ...and that's it.

That's better than nothing, but it doesn't actually solve the problem. If you already know you sleep badly, what do you do next?

Most companies stop at tracking because it's easy — collecting data is simpler than doing something about it.

---

## The New Model: AI Enables Real-Time Intervention

Advances in AI and miniaturized biosensors have changed everything. Now we can:
1. **Read your brain activity in real-time using EEG on a wearable device
2. **Detect when you're struggling to fall asleep
3. **Deliver personalized stimulation** to help your brain relax into deeper sleep

This isn't science fiction anymore — it's what we've built into the DreamPilot M103.

### Why AI is the key here
Every brain is different. Generic white noise works for some people, but not everyone. AI can adapt to *your* sleep patterns — it learns what works for you over time, and adjusts stimulation accordingly.

---

## Privacy: Can You Have AI Sleep Without Giving Up Your Data?

One big concern we hear all the time: "If it's AI, does it have to send all my sleep data to the cloud?"

The good news is: **no, it doesn't**.

Modern AI models can run completely on-device. Our device works fully offline — all the processing happens right on the mask, and your sleep data never has to leave your phone if you don't want it to.

You get the benefits of AI intervention without the privacy tradeoff. That's a design choice we made intentionally — we think privacy shouldn't be a luxury you have to pay extra for.

---

## What This Means For You

If you've tried everything and still wake up tired, AI sleep technology might be what you're looking for. Here's what to look for when you shop:

1. **Does it actually do anything, or just track?** — If it only gives you a score in the morning, keep looking.
2. **Can you use it offline?** — If it requires constant WiFi and cloud sync, think twice about privacy.
3. **Is it priced for everyday use?** — You don't have to pay a monthly subscription just to sleep better.
