# SELEPU M103: The Technology Behind AI-Powered Sleep Wellness

The global sleep wellness market has grown rapidly, yet most products still fail to address the root cause of poor sleep: how our brains transition through sleep stages. At SELEPU, we've developed a fundamentally different approach with our M103 AI Sleep Wellness Device — and today we want to share the technology that makes it possible.

## The Problem We're Solving

Modern life has created a sleep crisis. Stress, screen exposure, irregular schedules, and aging populations have led to widespread sleep difficulties:

- 2.6 billion people globally experience sleep issues
- Sleep disorders cost the global economy $400+ billion annually
- Traditional solutions (medications, supplements, basic devices) often have limited effectiveness or unwanted side effects

What's been missing is an intelligent, personalized solution that works WITH the brain's natural sleep cycles rather than trying to override them.

## The SELEPU M103 Approach

The M103 is built on three core technological pillars:

### 1. Advanced EEG Monitoring
Our proprietary EEG sensors detect brain activity in real-time, identifying:
- Sleep latency (time to fall asleep)
- Sleep stages (light, deep, REM)
- Sleep quality indicators
- Awakening patterns

This isn't just monitoring for the sake of data — it's the foundation for personalized intervention.

### 2. AI-Powered Sleep Algorithms
Our DreamPilot AI engine processes EEG data and:
- Identifies the optimal moments for sleep intervention
- Personalizes stimulation patterns based on individual response
- Learns and improves with continuous use
- Provides actionable sleep insights

### 3. Multi-Sensory Stimulation
The M103 delivers precisely calibrated stimulation through multiple modalities:
- **Auditory:** Binaural beats and personalized soundscapes
- **Visual:** Red light therapy (660nm wavelength)
- **Tactile:** Gentle vibration patterns
- **Thermal:** Optional hot/cold compress functionality
